`make crafts` draws all four at 90 px/m, where the rider is about 160 px
tall: enough to see that he is wrong, never enough to see WHY. Pipe one
craft big instead —
`node --experimental-strip-types scripts/craft-preview.mjs --craft dart
--scale 300 --out <path>` — and read the BOW and STERN cells: a monotone
limb taper, a facet count that reads as plates, a gap at the lower back
and a helmet with no chin bar are all obvious there and all invisible on
the sheet.

The sheet is still the before/after artifact the PR owes; the big render
is the diagnosis. `--scale` and `--out` are the lab's own flags, so no
tooling is needed — but the Read tool downsamples anything wider than
2000 px, so crop the region rather than rendering the whole strip larger.
