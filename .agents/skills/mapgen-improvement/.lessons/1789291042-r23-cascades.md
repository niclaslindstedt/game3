`course.radius` is quoted by name in three other places and implied in two
more. Raising it from 55 m to 80 m took, in this order:

- `leg.round` — R25's rounding was drawn at 58–76 m, UNDER the old floor, which
  made the mark the tightest corner of the course on 79 levels out of eighty.
  It has to clear the new floor.
- `leg.offshore` and `leg.span` — both are DERIVED from `round`, so both bands
  are re-measured from what the generator actually produces, not guessed.
- `circuit.lap`, `circuit.turn` and `circuit.swing` — the polar loop is scaled
  to its lap length, so its tightest corner scales with it. At R23 = 80 the raw
  loop draw survived 17% of the time against 90% before, and seeds started
  throwing; a longer lap is what buys it back.
- The POPULATION's build-time bar. The basin's stamp grows with the square of
  how far the leg reaches, so a wider rounding is a slower generator:
  `leg.round` 88–108 took the 30-seed mean from 650 ms to 714 and failed
  `mapgen_population_test`'s 900 ms bar under a parallel vitest run; 84–100
  came back to 629. Measure with the population's OWN seeds
  (`i * 53 + 7`) — a different sample said the generator had got faster.

And `search.attempts` is the tail's knob: `generateLevel` THROWS when it runs
out, which is a hard failure on a seed a player can share. Roughly one seed in
150 hit the old bound of 24 — on `main` as well — so check the failure rate
over a few hundred seeds on both coasts whenever a rejection reason gets more
common, rather than only on the test corpus.
