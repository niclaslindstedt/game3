Draw the generated lap underneath the gate-to-gate path through each single-buoy pass point. The separation is the visual proof that following the natural bend cannot collect the checkpoint. Clamp two-line buoy labels inside the map and give them a translucent paper backing; off-course marks naturally sit at the image edge, where an unclamped label hides the color/side instruction the schematic exists to explain.
