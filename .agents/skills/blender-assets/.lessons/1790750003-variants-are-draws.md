The trees taught it for the woods; the undergrowth and the rocks are the
same arithmetic with more kinds. Every variant of a modelled kind is its own
instanced mesh, so a coast with ten undergrowth rows costs +30 draws a pass
at four variants, twice that with the mirror live, and a rock kind +3.
The undergrowth was given four variants and, for its far band, THE CODE'S
OWN SHAPE past 35 m (`UNDER_FULL`) — drawn whole to the reach the karst's
cruise frame doubled, 0.89M to 1.95M triangles, because a coast plants
thousands of these and most stand past 35 m — the bushes ~24 clusters (a
big row) or ~10 (a small one)
laid on a spiral by surface area — rings by height read as a stack of
hedges — and the reeds one leaf a cane and two sprays a plume, to stay
inside about 2–3× the code's triangles. Measure with `make profile
ARGS="--biome …"` on and off before deciding a fifth variant is worth it.
