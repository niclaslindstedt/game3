title: The water bends for the trail
---

The trail behind the craft now MOVES the sea rather than only whitening it. Behind a transom running dry the surface drops away; a couple of hull lengths back, where the water thrown out to either side closes again and the jet's stream lands on it, it sprouts into a mound standing proud of the still line; and from there the raised water rides outward at Kelvin's angle as two long arms, so what a rider looks back at is a triangle that never stops opening and fades into the sea a step at a time rather than stopping. The white wedge opens with it, out to most of the map instead of the near half.
