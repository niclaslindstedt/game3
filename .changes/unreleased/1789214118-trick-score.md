title: A score for the air and the flips over it
---

The clock is the race; now there is a second game on the same water. Time off the water pays by the second at a rate that rises with the flight, so an ordinary jump is worth something and the twenty seconds the open ocean throws you for is worth a hundred and fifty times it. Every whole revolution nose-over-tail is scored the instant it closes, in the air — and the second one of a flight is worth twice the points and twice the rung, so a double backflip pays ×4 rather than ×3. It all rides on one combo that stays open across a landing, banks a second after you stop flying, and is gone entirely if you capsize, bury the bow or put yourself back at a gate. The combo counts up over the nose under the air clock and beats each time it wins a multiplier; the run's total sits with the clock in the corner.
