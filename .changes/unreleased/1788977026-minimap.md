type: Added
title: A minimap of the course
---

The HUD's top-right corner now carries a minimap, adapted from the sibling rally game's: a square of sea seen from above with the craft in the middle of it, the shoreline and the shallows around it, every rock and reef drawn apart, the racing line, and the gates with the one you owe picked out — and a chevron riding the frame when that gate is off the window. The frame itself is the gauge, filling clockwise with the gates reached, and the window opens and closes with the speedo so what it holds is a constant amount of warning rather than a constant piece of water.
