type: Added
title: The sea life, modelled
---

Every animal under every coast's water is now modelled in Blender off its own row of the catalog and drawn from a committed model, its hide still painted by the game's own rule and its tail beaten by the same shader: a smooth body lofted through the catalog's girth with a keel and a snout, an eye each side, a dorsal with a curved trailing edge, rounded pectorals, a fish's second dorsal and anal fin, a shark's tall upright lobe, a whale's notched flukes, and a ray's whole body a wing. A build can still draw the code-built animals (`VITE_MODEL_FAUNA=0`).
