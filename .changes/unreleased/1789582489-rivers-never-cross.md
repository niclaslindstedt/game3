title: Rivers no longer cross themselves
---

A river ran on inland from the race under a meander whose turning circle was one number, while its water was the corridor's — a hundred metres across at the mouth. A channel that wide bending on a thirty-eight metre radius cannot help meeting itself, and most seeds shipped one that did: a knot of water where two reaches had merged, and the centreline laid over its own channel like a road with an underpass. Twenty-seven taiga seeds in forty and eighteen mangrove.

Now the meander turns at a circle the channel allows — a couple of the water's own widths, never under the coast's floor — so the bends open out at the mouth and tighten into the creek at the head, the way a watercourse does; the taper is read off how far INLAND the water has got rather than off how far it has walked, so a reach that spends three hundred metres on one loop comes out of it the width it went in; and a walk that still runs into its own water is redrawn. No seed on either coast crosses now.
