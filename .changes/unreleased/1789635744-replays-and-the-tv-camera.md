title: Replays, and a TV camera that cuts to what you did
---

Every RACE, TRICKS run and TIME TRIAL is now recorded while you ride it, and watched back from the plate at the finish or from the pause card — where the row says what it costs, because watching the run so far ends it. What is kept is not a video: it is the controls you rode on and the shore you rode them on, so the replay is the game's own frames with the same physics doing the same thing, the whole field out there riding exactly the race it rode. A FREE ride keeps none, for the same reason it keeps no record.

A replay opens on the TV CAMERA — a rung you cannot reach while riding, because it is framed for somebody watching rather than somebody steering. It is a lens planted out on the water beside a moment the recording already knows is coming, so it cuts to the jump BEFORE the jump and the shot is a craft arriving into a frame that was already standing there. It cuts to what you actually did: a flight that stayed up, a flip or a spin or a corkscrew turned in one, another hull met at speed, and it going wrong. Over each of them the picture runs in slow motion, easing down into the moment and back out. Between them it hands the frame to the chase boom, flown rather than cut.

Nothing about a replay is written to disk — it lives as long as the tab does.
