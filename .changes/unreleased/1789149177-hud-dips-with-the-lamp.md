title: The HUD dips with the craft's lamp
---

The readouts were mixed to hold over a bright sea at noon, and over a coast lit by nothing but a headlamp and a moon that same white chrome was glare in front of the dark you were reading into — the chart in the corner brightest of all. Now the whole HUD comes down a stop as the sun goes: the ink off pure white, the navy behind every figure to nearly black, the chart's sand to slate. It rides the craft's own lamp rather than the clock, so the instruments can never disagree with the light you are riding by, and it comes on the way the lamp does — over the couple of minutes the sun takes to cross the horizon rather than in one frame. The warnings keep their colour, and so does the buoy orange of the gate you owe.
