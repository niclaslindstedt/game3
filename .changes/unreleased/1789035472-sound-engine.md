title: Sound — the engine, the pump, the water and every splash, synthesized
---

The run makes a noise. The engine's firing note and the pump's whine follow the revs the dial reads, the intake opens with the throttle, the pump froths on a launch from rest and the wet exhaust gurgles at idle; off a lip the crank runs free and the water goes quiet. The hull has its wash at displacement speed and its spray on the plane, the bottom slaps through a chop off the physics' own slam, the wind is in the rider's ears on the square of the apparent wind, the swell breathes under the hull and the surf breaks on the shore from the side it stands on. Every landing, dive, hit, grounding, capsize, launch, gate, ring, miss, reset and the finish has its own sound, and every splash leaves bubbles. The mix follows the camera. All of it is synthesized from authored parameters — the game ships no audio file — and OPTIONS has a SOUND fader for it, reading OFF at the bottom.
