type: Changed
title: Less text on the cards, and the help line at the foot of the page
---

The menus say less and say it where it can be read. The front door's wordmark
stands on its own — the tagline under it named two coasts, and there are three
already. RACE no longer fills or changes its word while it is held down: the
developer menu is meant to be found by somebody who knows where to press, not
advertised to everybody who rests a thumb on START. The level card's coast step
has dropped the line about where its shores came from. The CLASS row is now
drawn on a FREE RIDE's craft card alone, instead of standing on every other one
with a single chip nobody could press — every measured mode was already ridden
stock.

And the sentence explaining the row you are pointing at has left the card: it
floats at the foot of the WINDOW now, so it can be read while the pointer is
still on a row near the top. It used to sit at the bottom of a column taller
than the screen, which meant scrolling away from the row to read about it.
