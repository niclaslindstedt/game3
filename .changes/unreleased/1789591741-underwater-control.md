title: The rider keeps the craft under the water
---

A hull driven under is the rider's now: lean back with the gas on and the jet drives it out, lean forward and it goes deeper, and he is folded down behind the bars for as long as it lasts. He is given ten seconds under with the throttle open, or one second after letting it go — let go at any time and a second later the craft is turned up and brought to the surface the right way up, and a hull that comes out of a dive on its back is turned up the same way. A dive never ends in a capsize any more. On a tricks run the seconds under the water tick like the seconds in the air, on the same clock over the nose, and riding the craft back out clean under your own hands is the SUBMARINE, worth a step of multiplier — and it sells the air of the jump you dived out of, so a jump dived into and ridden back out is one combo. Being brought up by the water bails the combo the way a capsize does; the bow going in no longer does.
