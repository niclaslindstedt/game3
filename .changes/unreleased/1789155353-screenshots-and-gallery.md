title: Take a screenshot with ENTER, and keep them in a GALLERY
---

ENTER — or the new shutter under the minimap, for a screen with no keyboard — takes a picture of the frame you are looking at: the sea, the craft and the sky as the renderer drew them, the HUD over it exactly as it stood when you pressed, and the game's mark in the corner. The run never stops for it. The last forty pictures are kept in the browser on the machine they were taken on, and GALLERY on the front door is where they are looked at, flipped through, and shared, copied or saved to a file.
