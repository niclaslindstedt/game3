A second coast to ride: the MANGROVE — a warm, flat, salt shore of white sand and mangrove on a clear turquoise sea, with a long groundswell coming in over the sandbars, its own sky (a humid haze the cold coast never gets, and an afternoon storm for its squall), its own cover (the red mangrove standing in the water, cabbage and coconut palms, sea oats and sea grape, slash pine and live oak), its own birds (pelicans plunging, an osprey, egrets, ibis, the roseate spoonbill and a frigatebird hanging at height) and its own sea life (mullet leaping, snook and redfish along the mangrove edge, tarpon rolling, a stingray on the sand, the loggerhead and the manatee coming up to breathe, bottlenose dolphins, a bull shark and — one seed in fifty — a great hammerhead). The start card asks which COAST before which shore, `?biome=mangrove` is the link, and every lab takes `BIOME=`.

The TAIGA coast is now honestly what it is — a cold sheltered skerry coast: its water a grey-green rather than the brand palette's teal, its waves somewhat smaller (the islands break up the wind sea and take most of the ocean's swell), and the dolphin and the shark, which were never a cold sea's, have moved to the warm one.
