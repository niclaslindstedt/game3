title: Six arctic levels for the campaign, behind the taiga's table
---

The campaign runs a THIRD shore. Behind the cold coast's table — win the taiga
outright and it opens, the way the taiga opens behind the mangrove — are six
pinned polar levels, on the same rungs as the other two: MIDNIGHT SUN (bergs
either side of a flat line with the sun still up at one in the morning), SEA
SMOKE (two minutes of kickers in the fog that stands over every lead), FLOE
LAPS (three laps out past the glacier's front), NARROW LEAD, SNOW KICKERS (four
minutes of ramps in the snow with the sun going down for the year) and WHITE OUT
(nine metres of black water, growlers on the line, and a blizzard at last
light).

NARROW LEAD is the one no other coast can offer: the sea frozen right over, with
an icebreaker's channel cut down the course to ride and the sheet grounding a
hull that leaves it. It is also the tightest line on the coast, so the channel
is the whole of the ask.

Each of the six leads on something different — the bergs, the air, the distance,
the dark, the sky, the wind — and the shore climbs from end to end without a
wall. All six are open to RACE, TRICKS and TIME TRIAL as well, for the record
book, the moment the campaign opens the shore, and the coast has a banner of its
own on both cards.
