title: Pump the bars for the backflip
---

The lean-back answers to being WORKED as well as held. Hold it back going up a wave's face or up a ramp's deck and then TAP it off the lip: every tap is another haul on the bars, the hull comes round faster for each one, and the rider visibly throws himself further back as they land. A hold alone is the one committed pull it always was — enough to flip the stand-up and nobody else — so the taps are what bring a backflip round off an ordinary ramp, and how many it takes is the hull's: one or two on the stand-up, six or seven on the tourer, which is the rider's authority against the hull's pitch inertia and no knob of its own. Stop tapping and it stops turning faster, which is how the landing is aimed; the arcade's landing hand stands aside for the whole of a flight you are pumping, so it no longer unwinds the last quarter of a flip.
