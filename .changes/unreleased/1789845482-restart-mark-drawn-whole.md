title: The restart mark over the water is drawn whole
---

The arrow curling back on itself, on the left of the two round presses above the minimap, was clipped: its ring was sliced flat down the right-hand side and across the foot. Its box had been cut round the line the arc is drawn ON rather than round the stroke, which stands 5.5 units outside that line on every side — so the mark was trimmed rather than shrunk, and it now reads as the closed ring it was drawn as.
