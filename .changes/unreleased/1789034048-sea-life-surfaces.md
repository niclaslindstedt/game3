title: The sea life comes up where you can see it
---

Killer whales, porbeagles, dolphins and porpoises now roll up through the sea they are actually swimming in rather than a flat waterline, so the fin cuts the surface instead of being lost under the next wave, and a bull dolphin breaches clear of the water once or twice while you ride past his pod — dolphins are on about half the coasts now, up from one in fourteen.
