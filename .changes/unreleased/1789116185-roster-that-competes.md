type: Changed
title: Four craft that are four choices
---

The roster has been retuned so that picking a craft is a decision rather than a ranking. One of them used to hold the best acceleration and the best top speed at once and sit second in everything else, so the card's bars billed it as strictly better than the other three; now each craft owns exactly one of the four things it is billed on and pays for it somewhere else. The skiff leaves a buoy first, the marlin ends the straight first and needs the most room to come round, the otter is the hardest to unsettle and the slowest away, and the dart takes the tightest line there is with the lowest top end on the water.

They also actually race each other now. The turning circles used to span more than four to one, so gates — and with them the clock — went to whoever turned hardest and nothing else counted; they now span about two to one, every craft keeps the circle its archetype implies, and a full sweep finishes with under three km/h between the fastest and the slowest where it used to be thirteen.
