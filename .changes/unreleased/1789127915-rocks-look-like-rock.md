title: The rocks that stand out of the water are rocks
---

The sea stacks, the mark a course rounds and the skerries were tapered drums with flat lids — one shape a kind, spun and tinted, so a coast carried the same barrel at six sizes. Each is now carved for itself: a ragged plan that differs at every height, an axis that leans and kinks, its own taper and its own ledges, a crown broken off an apex rather than a lid, an undercut where the waves reach it, and patchy stone over the lot. And a sea stack now stands in the OPEN SEA where one is made, so no more chimneys up the middle of a river.
