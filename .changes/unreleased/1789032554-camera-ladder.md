type: Added
title: A ladder of six cameras
---

The camera key (and a new CAMERA button on the HUD) now walks six views instead of two, from the handlebars backwards: **BOW** out on the foredeck with the sea at eye level, **NOSE** over the bar, **CLOSE** over the transom, **CHASE** (the low Wave Race read the game is tuned to), **FAR** stood back for warning rather than drama, and **HELI** flown high and behind. The four outside views are one rig at four sizes, and they differ in weight as well as in place — the two nearest answer the hull briskly, the flown pair sway wide of a carve on a spring and settle back into it. Every one of them now hangs its boom along the FLIGHT PATH once the hull is off the water, its length unchanged, so a launch is watched from under the craft rather than from over the water it left. Walking the ladder is flown rather than cut. `?camera=heli` opens a run on any rung.
