title: A race costs the processor a fifth less
---

The waves a hull leaves in the water are read by every buoyancy probe of every craft on the water, a hundred and twenty times a second, and nine tenths of what that cost was working out which of a trail's few hundred ring packets were near enough to matter — repeated for each of a hull's two dozen probes, standing in one place. It is worked out once now, for a patch of water a hull long, and every probe in it reads the same short list. A twelve-craft race steps about a fifth faster; nothing about the water changed, and every simulated run comes back identical to the byte.
