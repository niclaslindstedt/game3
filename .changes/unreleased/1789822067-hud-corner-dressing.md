type: Changed
title: The HUD's two corners
---

The wind meter now stands with the rest of the bottom-left cluster instead of
out past the rev bar: the space the altitude figure needs is reserved by the
tape's own column, so the row it shares no longer has to be held open wide
enough for a reading it rarely prints. The two presses under the minimap are
discs rather than plates, lit from above and pressed into the panel under a
thumb — and the RESET goes to the warning colour and beats while a checkpoint
is owed, on the same heartbeat the chart puts round the gate itself.
