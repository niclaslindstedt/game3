title: The front door's drone is up from the first frame, and draws no course furniture
---

The menu's drone only took the lens once the front door's own card had reached the page, so the attract card — whose cover is opaque but which fades away — spent that fade revealing a flick of ordinary chase-cam footage, as though the game had glitched on its way up. The surface the app is opening on is now settled before the first shore is stood, and the attract card stands over the drone like every other card does. The guide line and the missed-checkpoint arrow are also gone from behind a card: they are instruments for a rider who is being guided, and from a lens a dozen storeys up with nobody steering they were a dotted line drawn across the sea.
