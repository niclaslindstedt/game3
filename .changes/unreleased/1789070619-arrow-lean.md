type: Changed
title: The arrow keys lean the rider
---

On the keyboard, the arrow cluster is now the handlebar: ← → steer and ↑ ↓ lean, with the same sign a thumb's bar has on a phone — pull toward you (↓) for nose up, push away (↑) for nose down. ↑ no longer opens the throttle; W is the throttle key, and Shift still leans forward alongside ↑.
