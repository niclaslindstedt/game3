title: The app's wave draws itself for a player who has asked for less motion
---

Asked to hold still, the game drew the wave on its attract card, its front door and its loading card as nothing but a faint ghost of itself, or drew it on anyway — the rule meant to park it finished was outweighed by the one that animates it and had never taken effect. It now stands finished, which is what the setting asks for: the information kept, the travelling gone.
