---
type: Changed
title: Tricks are asked for at the end of the axis, on the way up
---

Only a lean or a steer carried all the way to the end of its travel is a trick now, and the first one of a jump only counts while the craft is still rising — so trimming the nose over a wave or holding a line through a corner no longer throws an accidental flip, while a trick opened off the lip can still be worked the whole way down.
