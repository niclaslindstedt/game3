The presses drawn over a run — CAMERA, RESET, the minimap that opens the pause card, and the new-build mark — now answer a tap made while the other thumb is on the handlebar or the throttle, which on a phone is every tap that matters. A browser synthesises `click` from the FIRST finger on the glass alone, so with a control held every one of those buttons was hearing the touch and doing nothing with it; they are pressed from the pointer events instead, and the click that may follow no longer presses them a second time.
