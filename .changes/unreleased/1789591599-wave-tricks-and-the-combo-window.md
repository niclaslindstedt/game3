title: Three more tricks, and a combo that carries from one wave to the next
---

The score now pays for three things it did not. The CORKSCREW is a flip and a barrel roll turned in the SAME flight — a third element beside the two revolutions, worth more than the same two turns taken off two waves in a row, and lost with the rest if it is not landed. The WAVE RIDE is the top of a wave HELD: run along a wave with a metre or more in it, sit in the top tenth of it under way, and the score ticks by the second the way air time does, becoming an element of its own once it has been held a second. The LAYDOWN is the hull laid right over past forty-five degrees and brought back level — the one element that never leaves the water, and a lean away from a capsize the whole time.

And the combo stays open while the rider is IN PLAY: in the air, on the top of a wave, or driven under by a following wave with the craft still answering — and for a second on the water after any of them. So a trick off this wave links to a trick off the next at one multiplier instead of banking twice.
