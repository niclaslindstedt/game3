On the benchmark's card the COPY press squeezed the head's middle column to nothing, printing itself across the word BENCHMARK and wrapping the billing under it to four lines; it now says the short word on the card — with what it copies as its hover title — and the full sentence only on the wide surfaces that have room for it, while the scene breakdown in a copied report names the SKY and the RAIN instead of folding the frame's steepest per-pixel lever into a row called "everything else".
