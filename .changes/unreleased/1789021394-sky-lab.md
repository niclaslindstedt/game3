`make sky` — a lab that draws every weather against every hour on one coast as a single labelled contact sheet. The sky is the one part of the game a screenshot of a run cannot review: a seed is dealt one sky at one hour, so a picture of a run says whether that sky is wrong and nothing about the ladder it sits on, and a ladder is only ever judged side by side. Adapted from the sibling rally game's sky lab, with its harness-page pattern (`pwa/src/tools/`) brought across so the next lab that has to draw to answer its question is cheap to write.
