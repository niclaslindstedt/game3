title: Pause a run
---

Escape — or a press on the minimap, which is the way in on a phone — holds a run exactly where it stands and puts a pause card over it: the wave holds, the spray hangs and the clock waits, with the readouts still readable underneath. RESUME comes back on the very frame it left (a hold of any length costs the run nothing), OPTIONS is the front door's own page over the frozen frame — so the camera and the picture rows are judged on the water you actually stopped on — and MAIN MENU hands the craft back to the bot and comes back to the front door over the shore you were just on. Escape used to leave a run outright.
