title: The attract card hands over instead of cutting, and turns a ring instead of saying LOADING
---

The attract card's first beat no longer says LOADING under the house's name. What is happening there is worth a corner rather than a headline, so it is said the way a console says it: a small ring turning in the bottom right, clear of the lockup.

And the moment the game is standing, the card hands over rather than cutting. AGILATOR GAMES PRESENTS walks up out of the middle of the card to make room; the wave arrives in the space it left; the wave draws itself; SEA HAVEN rises under it; and the invitation to press arrives last, where the player was already looking.
