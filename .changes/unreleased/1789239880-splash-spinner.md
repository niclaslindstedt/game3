title: A turning ring on the attract card instead of the word LOADING
---

The attract card's first beat no longer says LOADING under the house's name. What is happening there is worth a corner rather than a headline, so it is said the way a console says it: a small ring turning in the bottom right, clear of the lockup. The invitation to press still lands in the middle of the card, where the player was already looking.
