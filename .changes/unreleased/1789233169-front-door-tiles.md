title: A front door of marks
---

The front door is a block of tiles two abreast instead of a column of full-width rows, each tile a drawn mark and a name — a buoy on START, a shutter on GALLERY, a mixing desk on OPTIONS and a prompt on DEVELOPER once it has been found. START keeps its orange and its seven-second hold, which still fills the tile it is being held on.
