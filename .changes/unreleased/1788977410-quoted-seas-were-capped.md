title: A sea quoted above six metres came out the same ten
---

The breaking clip held the surface against the arithmetic sum of its component amplitudes — the once-in-forever superposition where every wave crests together — instead of against the significant height the spectrum carries. Any sea quoted above about six metres was flattened to the same ten metres of wave: `?hs=8` and `?hs=50` rendered identically, and a twenty-metre storm photographed as a mirror. The depth limit is now Nelson's on the significant height, so the number asked for is the sea delivered wherever the water is deep enough to hold it.
