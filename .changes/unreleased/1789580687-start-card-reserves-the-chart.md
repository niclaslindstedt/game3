type: Fixed
title: The start card no longer grows when the seed's chart arrives
---

The box under the SHORE row now holds the chart's full square from the moment
the card opens, so the card stays exactly the height it will be while the
level is being built in the background. It used to be a good deal shorter
until the picture landed and then grow under the cursor — on a desktop the
card jumped by nearly 200 px and NEXT moved 94 px up the screen, which put a
different button under a press already aimed at one.
