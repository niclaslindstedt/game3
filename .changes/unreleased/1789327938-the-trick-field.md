title: A shore of ramps for a tricks run, and no rings over them
---

A tricks run is now ridden on a shore laid out for one: a line of ramps down the racing line and back up it, three to four times as many lips as the race course carries, spaced by exactly the water the catalog's most road-hungry hull needs to get back up to 95% of its top speed. No ring stands over any of them — a ring is a checkpoint, and a run with no course to count has nothing to check — so the rings and the buoys both go away for the run and only the lips are left.
