title: A finished run says so, and offers the three ways on
---

A run that reached the finish line or the buzzer put a figure on screen and
nothing else. The craft coasts from that moment on — it is the run being over
— but with nothing on the plate saying what to do about it, a finish read as
the game freezing, and the only way on was Escape and the pause card.

The plate now says RUN FINISHED over the figure and carries the three presses
under it: RIDE AGAIN, WATCH REPLAY where the run left a recording, and MAIN
MENU. All three are buttons the game already had — RIDE AGAIN is the B key's
own line and the other two are the pause card's — so nothing new was added to
press, only a second way to reach them, and a thumb can reach them at last.

It also stands in a layer of its own now, so switching the readouts off no
longer takes the only way off a finished run away with them. And the gold rim
a new record used to earn has started painting again.
