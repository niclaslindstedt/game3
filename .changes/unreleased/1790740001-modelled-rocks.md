title: The boulders, the erratics and the reefs, modelled
---

The rocks the coast strews — the boulders at the waterline, the blocks the ice left on the beach and the reefs under the surface — are now modelled in Blender in four variants each and drawn from committed models in place of the one sphere and the one die the code spun: whalebacks, cracked and ridged boulders and pairs; blocks, tilted slabs and split blocks; platforms, ledges and broken reefs, every face its own tone. A build can still draw the code's lumps (`VITE_MODEL_ROCKS=0`).
