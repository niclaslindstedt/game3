Every benchmark run is now kept and listed under DEVELOPER ▸ BENCHMARK HISTORY with the picture settings that produced each score on its own line as a bar a row, so two runs with one row moved sit side by side without anybody having had to plan for it — a row opens that run's graph back up full screen with its debug report beside it, and the whole sheet copies as text with a legend under it.
