type: Added
title: The birds, modelled
---

Every bird over every coast is now modelled in Blender off its own row of the roster and drawn from a committed model, flapped and folded by the same shader: a chest deep under the shoulders, the neck curving up to a head with a brow, a bill that comes to a point, wings with a rounded leading edge and a little camber, notched into primaries on the broad-winged birds, a tail fanned into feathers, and legs trailing where a species carries them. A build can still draw the code-built birds (`VITE_MODEL_BIRDS=0`).
