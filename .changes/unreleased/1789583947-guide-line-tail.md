title: The guide line no longer blinks out as you cross a checkpoint
---

The dashed line in the water hung its tail off the checkpoint just taken, so the moment you crossed a mark the dashes astern of the hull vanished and grew back over the next few boat lengths — the mark faltering at the one moment you are looking at it hardest. The tail now hangs off the checkpoint before last and simply runs through the crossing.
