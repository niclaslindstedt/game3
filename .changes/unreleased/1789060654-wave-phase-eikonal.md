The sea no longer stands still in a short sideways chop in the lee of a reef or either side of a river mouth. Each wave's phase over the level is now solved as a proper wavefront (the eikonal equation over the sea bed) instead of being integrated along one heading, so the waves keep their length and keep moving everywhere the sea reaches, shorten and turn toward the shore as they come in, and bend in round headlands and through a river mouth as arcs about the corner; the surface's slope and the water's motion follow the turned crests.
