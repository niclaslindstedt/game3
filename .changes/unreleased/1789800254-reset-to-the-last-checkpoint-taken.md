type: Changed
title: The reset goes back to the last checkpoint you TOOK, and hands the missed stretch back
---

RESET used to stand you behind the last checkpoint the run was DONE with — which counted one you had gone past and paid five seconds for, so the one thing it could not do was get you a checkpoint you had missed. Now it stands you behind the last one you actually threaded and rewinds the run to it: every checkpoint charged since comes off the tally, its penalty comes back off the clock, and the stretch is owed again in order. Miss one, press R, ride the stretch again and take it this time. It is not a free undo — it reaches back no further than a checkpoint the run was approved at, so a miss you rode on from and then took a later checkpoint after stays paid for, and what it costs you either way is riding that water a second time from a standing start.
