title: The water reflects the sky and the sun
---

The sea is lit pixel by pixel now rather than vertex by vertex: every wave
face reflects the sky it is actually pointing at — a sunset lays its orange
along the faces turned toward it and leaves the backs blue, a squall's black
ceiling sits on the water with its lit rim at the skyline — the sun glints
in it as a road of sparkle from a low sun and a polite glitter from a high
one, the wind's ripples roughen the surface without moving it, a crest with
the sun behind it glows green, and foam is streaks with edges rather than a
white tint. The sun itself has a glare round its disc. Any seed can be
looked at under any light: `?hour=` and `?weather=` on the URL (and
`HOUR=`/`WEATHER=` on `make screenshots`) ride it at that hour under that
sky.
