type: Added
title: The crafts and the rider, modelled
---

Every craft and the rider are now modelled in Blender off the game's own numbers and drawn from committed models: each hull stands on the physics' own station lines, with a smooth gel coat under the sky, a creased chine and keel, a proper saddle, grab handle, pump and nozzle — and its bars now turn with the nozzle, the rider's hands with them. The rider is one body that bends at the joints, in his vest, wetsuit, knee pads and helmet. A build can still draw the code-built crafts and rider (`VITE_MODEL_CRAFTS=0`, `VITE_MODEL_RIDERS=0`).
