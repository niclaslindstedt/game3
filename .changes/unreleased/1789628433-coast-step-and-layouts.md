---
type: Changed
title: The coast is a card of its own, with the game's own picture of it on it
---

The CAMPAIGN and the level card (RACE, TRICKS, TIME TRIAL) now ask in two
steps: which COAST, then which water on it. The coast step is a row per
shore, the width of the card, with a render of that coast behind it — taken
by the game itself from the helicopter seat on the first level that shore
opens on, under that level's own day — panning slowly across the row, with a
padlock and a reason on a shore the campaign has not opened yet. CONTINUE and
RIDE stay in the head on both steps, so nobody has to choose a coast to find
where they stopped.

And every level box now draws the LAYOUT of its own racing line behind what
it says: the whole course as a shape, so six boxes read as six different
rides before a name on any of them has been read, a lapped shore reads as the
loop it is, and a shore still behind a padlock shows the shape of what is on
the other side of it.
