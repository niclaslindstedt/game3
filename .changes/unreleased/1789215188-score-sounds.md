title: The score can be heard
---

The trick score has a voice. Every revolution you close in the air latches with a chime that climbs a minor third for each turn of the same flight, so the second half of a double backflip is heard as the second and not as a repeat — and it comes through clean, because off the lip the pump has no water in it and the top of the mix is empty. A second after you are back on the water the combo pays: a rising triad, as loud and as long as the purse was and as high as the multiplier it was taken at, so a one-second hop is a tick under the engine and a double backflip is the loudest news on the water. Go over the bars instead and it drains away — two falling squares and a sine sliding a long way down, dry, with no echo on it at all.
