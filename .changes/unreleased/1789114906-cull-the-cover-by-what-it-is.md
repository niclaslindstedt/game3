The shore's cover is culled by what each plant IS rather than by one radius
for the whole roster: a species is drawn while it still stands tall enough in
the frame to tell from the shore behind it, so the trees keep the whole of the
view and the reed, the grass, the heather and the shingle stop where they
stopped being visible. The water's mirror gets a shorter reach again, since it
draws into a fraction of the frame's pixels. The DISTANCE row's own radii are
pulled in to sit just past the fog each stop closes at instead of hundreds of
metres beyond it. Together that is around half the triangles out of a frame on
the coast, with the picture unchanged at every camera.
