The wake is drawn by the water itself. The craft's trail — the jet's white road, the boil at the transom, the V of aerated water spreading either side at Kelvin's angle with the speed, the transom's hollow and the fan's crest — is rasterised into a map round the craft each frame, and the water shader reads it: the road is foam in the same light and off the same tile as a whitecap, the churn bends the sky the water reflects, and the surface itself dips and lifts for the hull. A landing's foam goes into the same map, and the spray's droplets are lit by the same two lights as the water they were thrown off.
