title: Three ways onto the water — RACE, TRICKS and TIME TRIAL
---

The start card's first row is now the MODE. RACE stands eleven others on the grid with you — the whole roster, at your class — holds the field under three lights and lets it go on one GO; the hulls can be leaned on, your place reads beside the clock, and the last gate says where you finished. TRICKS is the same shore with the course taken off it: no gates, the ramps left standing, a clock that runs DOWN from the two, four or six minutes you give it and a score that runs up, stock craft only. TIME TRIAL is the race with the field taken off. The best this machine has seen on each shore in each mode is kept and shown under the start card's chart, and the plate at the end of a run says NEW BEST when you beat it. `?mode=` and `?minutes=` carry both rows in a link.
