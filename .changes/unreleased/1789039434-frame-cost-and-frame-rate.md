title: A lighter frame — the shore culled to what the lens sees, the water sampled only where it shows, and a FRAME RATE row

The frame costs less. The shore's cover now stands in tiles, so the trees behind the camera and off to the side are not drawn at all — on a phone that was most of the frame's triangles, every frame, whatever the camera was looking at. The near water grid samples the engine's sea only for the vertices the lens can see, which is close to half the frame's JavaScript given back with no change to the picture. OPTIONS ▸ VIDEO gains a **FRAME RATE** row — 30, 60 or MAX — that holds the game to a rate the machine can keep, so a phone that draws unevenly draws evenly instead; the engine steps the same seconds behind it. `?fps=` and the labs' `--fps` name the new row the way the other picture rows are named.
