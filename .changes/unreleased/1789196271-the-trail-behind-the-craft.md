title: The trail behind the craft
---

The wake now opens the whole way behind you and stays white where it leaves the frame, breaking into patches rather than fading out a few lengths back; it is two diverging rails at the transom that fill in with broken white as they age, its edge breaks into crescents rather than a ruled line, a carve throws the wash wide to the OUTSIDE of the turn, and the rails astern throw fine drops into the air so the trail has volume as well as a surface. Landing no longer stretches a wedge of wake back across the air you just flew — a flight is a clean break in the trail, and a hard landing throws its mark sideways instead.
