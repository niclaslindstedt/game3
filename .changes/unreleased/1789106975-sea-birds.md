title: Birds over the water
---

The sky over the coast has birds in it: gulls wheeling off the skerries and standing on the buoys, terns plunging for fish in the shallows, cormorants beating low in lines and drying their wings on the rocks, rafts of eider in the lee of a skerry that get up off the water when the craft runs at them, and a sea eagle soaring off the tallest pine. In spring and autumn skeins of geese, swans and cranes cross high overhead — north in spring, south in autumn — and everything goes to roost as the sun goes down.
