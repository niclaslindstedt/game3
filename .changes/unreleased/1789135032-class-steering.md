---
type: Changed
title: A faster class no longer steers like a different craft
---

The speed class used to scale the pump and say nothing to the controls: because every steering term is quoted against the water in absolute metres a second, OPEN handed you four times the yaw acceleration off the same flick of the bars, a hull that came round _sharper_ per metre of track the faster you rode it, and a lean off a ramp that rotated faster and hung half again as long — twitchy enough to spin on a gate line and to loop off every jump. The rider's nozzle angle and his authority in the air now come back down with the class, so one craft's classes all manoeuvre the same and a faster class turns a little wider rather than tighter. STOCK is untouched: nothing about the roster at class 1 has moved.
