title: The game says when a new build is ready
---

A new build deployed while the tab is open now announces itself, and as quietly as a thing can and still be there: a small mark at the end of the top bar, no card and no plate across the water. One press arms it into the word RELOAD, a second restarts the page onto the new build, and it disarms itself after a few seconds — so a stray tap costs nothing and ignoring it costs less than dismissing it would.
