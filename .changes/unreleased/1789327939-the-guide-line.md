title: A line in the water to whatever you are riding at
---

A run of small white dashes lies just under the sea surface, running from the bow along the racing line to the next checkpoint — or, in a tricks run, to the next lip ahead of you. They bank with the wave face they are under and ride over a crest the way anything floating there would, so what says where to go is in the water you are already looking at rather than in a corner of the glass. It rides with the HUD's own switch.
