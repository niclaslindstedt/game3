Sea Haven exists: a personal-watercraft racing game in the browser — one generated taiga shore with its sea, its skerries, its buoy gates and the floating ramps that throw you through the air gates, one craft to ride it on, a chase camera, a HUD with the speed, the revs, the clock and the wind, keyboard and touch controls, installable as an offline-capable PWA.
