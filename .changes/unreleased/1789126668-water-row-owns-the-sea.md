type: Changed
title: The WATER option now owns the whole sea
---

WATER is the whole sea in one word: the grid the surface is carried on, and everything drawn on it — the spray off the hull, the wake behind it, what a landing knocks into the water, and what the water mirrors. Those four moved off DETAIL, which now covers the world around the water: the sea life, the tree line, the cloud and the rain. Turning DETAIL up can no longer spend back the frames a low WATER was meant to save — DETAIL at HIGH over WATER at LOW is a thick shore under a heavy sky over the same cheap water, and on the cruise scene that is 45% fewer draw calls and 42% fewer triangles a frame than the same two rows bought before.
