type: Changed
title: The benchmark's breakdown accounts for the whole frame
---

DEVELOPER ▸ BENCHMARK's report used to quote two frame rates a few percent apart — one in its headline and another under its breakdown — because the breakdown billed only the span of the work and not the frame's whole period. The gap between frames is now a line of its own, so both numbers are the rate the machine actually scored. The largest line in that breakdown used to be `rest`, standing for "the scene posed, culled and retoned" without timing any of the three; those are three lines now, and what is left over is the clock's rounding. And where the mirror only draws on some frames — which is what REFLECTIONS ▸ GLOW asks for — the draw calls and the triangles are stated for both kinds of frame instead of being averaged into a frame that was never drawn.
