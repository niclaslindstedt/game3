type: Changed
title: Bigger seas
---

The sea runs half a metre of significant height even in the lee of the shore and near two metres at the seaward bound in a strong wind — the wind now blows a fresh 6–14 m/s — so a run at pace is a climb, a launch and a drop into the next face, and a run can be handed a sea outright (`?hs=20` stands a twenty-metre storm, which the model carries out to the horizon).
