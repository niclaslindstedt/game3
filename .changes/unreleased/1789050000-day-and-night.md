title: Time passes — a day and a night in twenty-four minutes, in four seasons
---

The sun moves: one minute of riding is one hour of sun, so a run started at sunset rides into the twilight and then the dark, the full moon comes up over the sea and lays its road across the water, the stars and the Milky Way come out, and twenty-four minutes on the water is the whole of a day and a night, dawn mist and all. The craft carries a headlamp and a red and a green sidelight that the sky switches on as the sun touches the water, the headlamp's pool is on the sea and the hull alike, and the buoys and the rings light their own caps after dark, so a night course can be found by them and by the minimap. Every level is dealt a SEASON — spring, summer, autumn or winter, the Bothnian coast's own as the weather service dates them — and the start card has a SEASON row (`?season=`) beside TIME: how long the day is and how dark the night gets are the season's, so a taiga summer night never gets past twilight and an October one is black under the moon, and the water is the season's temperature.
