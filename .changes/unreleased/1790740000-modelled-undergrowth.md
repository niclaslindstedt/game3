type: Added
title: The shore's undergrowth, modelled
---

Every bush, tuft of grass, reed and piece of loose stone on every coast is now modelled in Blender off its own row of the roster and drawn from committed models, in four variants a kind: sallows and sea grapes as a stool of shoots under a crown of leaf clusters, junipers as shaggy columns, the heather and the maquis as bumpy mats, grass with creased blades arching over (some in seed), reed beds as canes with a leaf and a feathered plume, and the shingle, the shell and the stranded ice as faceted lumps. A build can still draw the code-built ones (`VITE_MODEL_UNDERGROWTH=0`).
