title: Sea life under see-through water
---

The water is semi-transparent now — clear over the shallows, solid toward the horizon — and there is something under it. Ten animals live on the taiga coast, each as rare as it deserves to be: schools of Baltic herring, roach and perch on every ride, a pike lying alone over a weed bed, sea trout, and then the ones worth the ride — a harbour porpoise every few coasts, white-beaked dolphins, a porbeagle, a pod of killer whales about one seed in forty, and a minke whale about one in sixty. They swim their own beat, they are drawn to be read from above (the dorsal, the flippers, the tail, an orca's white saddle, a minke's white flipper bands), the cetaceans roll their backs through the surface to breathe, and a deep one is a pale ghost where one near the surface is crisp. `make level SEED=n` prints the roster a seed drew.
