---
type: Changed
title: The roster carries its weight further aft, so the bow rides over a following sea
---

Every craft now carries its centre of gravity at 12% of its own length aft of mid rather than 10%. At rest the hulls sat a fraction BOW-DOWN and planed at about 2°, the very bottom of the band the planing model is fitted over; they now rest level to a degree bow-up and plane at 2.1–2.7°. What set the number is riding WITH the waves: trimmed any further forward the hull is swallowed by the wave it overtakes, the flow angle over the bottom goes negative, the planing lift stops firing and the craft wallows at a third of its speed. Measured flat out down the wave train, mean speed rises from 38 to 42 km/h and the time spent with the whole bottom under the water falls from a fifth of the run to an eighth. Every craft is a little faster flat out (1–1.5 km/h) and rides a little higher, so there is more air off the same ramp.
