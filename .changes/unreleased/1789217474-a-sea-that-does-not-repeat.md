type: Changed
title: The calm sea stops reading as corduroy
---

The wave field is drawn from its spectrum differently, and open water no longer arrives as a set of long parallel ridges evenly spaced down the wind. Each component now takes its heading THROUGH the directional spread rather than being weighted against it, so no wave is robbed of its share by where it happens to point and no one wave carries half the sea; the fan is narrowest at the peak and opens away from it, the way a real wind sea's is, so short chop rides confused over ordered swell; and the band is sampled twice as finely, which is what pushes the pattern's repeat out past the water a rider can see. Same sea to ride — same height, same steepness, same faces — and a sea that looks like one from the saddle.
