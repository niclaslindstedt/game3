A fourth coast to ride: the KARST — a warm-temperate limestone shore standing high and steep over the clearest, bluest sea there is, with white pebble coves in the bays between the headlands, more islets, reefs and stacks off it than any coast, a gorge for its river, a short steep wind sea that comes off the land and no ocean swell at all, its own skies (a calm's heat haze, the southerly's veil, lid and rain, and the summer thunderstorm off the mountains for its squall), its own cover (Neptune grass under the water, pebbles on the tideline, samphire on the rock, the maquis — sage, rosemary, immortelle, rockrose, tree spurge, myrtle, prickly juniper — the coastal pine leaning over the slab, holm oak and olive on the red soil, the cypress standing up black, and giant reed and oleander along the gorge), its own birds (the yellow-legged and Audouin's gulls, the shag, two shearwaters out over the deep, the common tern, screaming parties of swifts round the cliffs, Eleonora's falcon, the griffon vulture, the hooded crow, and the storks and cranes going over), its own sea life (sardine shoals, bream and bass over the pebbles, garfish skittering along the surface, dentex off the reef, the loggerhead and the bottlenose, amberjack, striped dolphins leaping, bluefin busting bait, the sunfish lying flat on the surface, the swordfish and the blue shark finning, the fin whale far out — and, once in a blue moon, a monk seal out of its cave), its own grade (gold on the stone, ultramarine in the shadows), and two new cries in the bank for the swift and the crow. `?biome=karst` is the link, and every lab takes `BIOME=karst`. And the campaign has a fourth shore behind the polar one's table — KARST, six levels: BLUE WATER, HEAT HAZE, ISLET LAPS, SOUTHERLY, LIMESTONE KICKERS and BLACK WALL — which RACE, TRICKS and TIME TRIAL ride for the record book too.
