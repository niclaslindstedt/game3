title: The developer menu is opened by holding the craft, and the craft says so
---

The seven-second hold that lets the developer menu out has moved off the front door's RACE tile and onto the turning hull on the craft card — and it now answers. Hold the craft for seven seconds and it whips round twice and settles back into its slow spin, which is the card saying the hold landed; DEVELOPER is waiting on the front door's foot strip afterwards, as before. The old hold fired in complete silence on a tile that also starts a run, so there was no way to tell it from a tile that had stopped working.
