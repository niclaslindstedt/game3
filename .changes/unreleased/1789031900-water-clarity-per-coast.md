title: Water you can read the sea life through, and a bottom that stays hidden
---

The taiga's water is less see-through, and what it stops showing is the sea
bed rather than what swims over it. A coast's water is now a row of its own —
its tones, how far they run with depth, how opaque the surface is, and how far
the eye gets into it — so a warmer shore can be dealt clearer, lighter water
when one is built. The bottom fades into an unlit tone over that reach and
past it has no shape left to give it away, while a school near the surface
still reads against it.
