title: The campaign
---

A CAMPAIGN tile on the front door, first of the ways onto the water: two shores of six pinned levels — the warm mangrove coast, then the cold taiga behind it — every level a fixed seed on a fixed day (its hour, its season, its sky, its wind and the swell standing off the coast), named for what it is like rather than where it is, climbing from a metre of glass water to a nine-metre sea in the winter dark. Four races and two tricks runs a shore, a two-lap circuit among them. Every level is ridden against the same eleven riders, there to be raced among and never leaned on — the hulls pass through each other. A level pays the podium three, two and one for the whole field; the next level opens behind a podium (on a tricks level, behind a bronze, silver or gold medal — a score to beat), and the next shore behind the table. The board keeps the better afternoon of every level and is never spent. The start card for a race, a time trial and a tricks run now asks only which coast and which shore — those modes ride the day the shore deals — and the day's rows are the free ride's alone.
