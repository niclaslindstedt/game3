title: The stream behind the nozzle
---

The pump's stream is now visible at speed, not just off a standing start: a thin bright tongue straight out of the nozzle, narrow where it leaves and opening over the couple of hull lengths behind the craft. Making room for it fixed the reason it could never be seen — the boil's bulb was timed rather than placed, so at 90 km/h the "bulb right behind the transom" was still most of the way there ten metres back and the whole near wake was one flat band of white. And the road no longer draws a hard bright line down the middle of the wake for as far as the trail runs: it is brightest just astern of the craft and settles into the broken wedge behind it, the way an aerial photograph has it.
