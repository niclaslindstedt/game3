title: The jet comes first
---

Open the throttle from a dead stop and the pump now blasts a stream of broken water astern before the craft has moved — a tongue on the water and a stream of drops in the air over it — where the sea used to stay glassy until the hull was already doing 16 km/h. The jet hands the water over to the trail exactly as the trail goes white, so the two cross without either appearing out of nothing.
