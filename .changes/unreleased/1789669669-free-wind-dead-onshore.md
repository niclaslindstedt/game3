---
type: Changed
title: A free ride's wind always blows straight in off the sea
---

FREE's start card no longer asks which quarter the wind blows from: it is always dead onshore, square on to the average line of the shore. The row it replaces was the only one on the card that could quietly undo the two above it — the quarter is what the fetch is measured along, so a wind turned off the sea has no water at its back and grows nothing, and a rider who set forty metres a second and a twenty-metre swell and then dragged the quarter round got flat water with nothing on the card saying which answer had cancelled which. The sea a free ride asks for is now the sea it gets. The `?windfrom=` link parameter and the screenshot lab's `--windfrom` flag go with the row.
