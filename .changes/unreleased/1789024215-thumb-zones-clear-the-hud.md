title: The throttle no longer eats a press meant for the HUD
---

On a phone the two thumb zones now stop at the lower three fifths of the screen instead of climbing to just under the top bar, so a press aimed at the readouts' own buttons — RESET, and the new-build mark that hangs under the cluster in portrait — reaches the button instead of opening the throttle. The readouts also sit above the zones outright, so a button stays pressable even where the two would overlap.
