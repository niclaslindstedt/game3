title: The reset and camera presses are half the minimap each
---

The two marks over the water — back to the last gate, and the next camera — are sized at half the minimap's width apiece rather than a third, so the pair comes to the plate's own edge and the top-right corner reads as one block. They were sized for a row of three when the shutter shared it; the shutter went to a key and a menu row, and the divisor stayed behind.
