title: The four craft are four kinds of machine
---

Each craft is now a named archetype — a runabout, a supercharged musclecraft, a tourer and a freestyle stand-up — with the hardware to match: a blower that only pushes at the top of the rev range, a reverse bucket, nozzle trim on the rider's lean, and its own sponson bite, ride plate and bow rocker.
