title: The birds have voices
---

The birds over the coast are heard as well as seen: gulls crying off the skerries and over the shallows, terns shrieking over the fish, drakes cooing on a raft with the engine at idle beside it, and the skeins of geese, swans and cranes calling faintly from high overhead as they go over. A raft the craft runs at gets up with a whirr of wings and a burst of shouts. Every cry is synthesized like everything else, sized by how far off the bird is and panned to where it stands, quieter than the smallest splash, and dealt off the seed so a ride cries the same cries twice. The cormorant and the eagle keep quiet.
