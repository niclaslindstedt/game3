type: Changed
title: Eight levels a shore, one race one tricks run all the way up
---

Every campaign shore now runs EIGHT levels instead of six, and the two games alternate the whole way up it: a race, a tricks run, a race, a tricks run, four of each. The lapped circuit is still the third rung — it is a kind of track, not a third game — and the finale is now a tricks run, six minutes of air over the worst water its coast has: a squall sea off the warm shore, a winter storm among the granite, twenty metres of swell on the polar one and a broken sea on the limestone one. Eight new shores were curated for it, two a coast — a three-minute rung in the middle of each climb and the six-minute finale on the end — and the tricks runs now lengthen as they go: two minutes, three, four, six. Every rung still asks more than the one before it.

A board from the old six-rung ladder is moved onto the new one rather than dropped: what was rung four, five and six on a shore is rung five, six and seven now, the same water in the same game, and the two new rungs start open.
