title: The ocean has swell in it
---

Every wave in the game used to be one this coast's own wind grew, and that is a short sea — at the lightest wind the generator deals it is 1.2 m high with a 28 m wavelength, which under a 3.5 m hull reads as texture rather than as ocean. A coast now also carries GROUNDSWELL: long, slow, nearly unidirectional waves from weather a thousand kilometres away, arriving in sets of three or four with flatter water between them, standing wherever the open sea reaches and nothing up a river, shoaling and turning as they come in over the bed. It is the part of the sea the local wind did not make, so a calm morning still has big water outside the shore — and it costs nothing a frame, because the three components it needs came out of the wind chop's tail.
