title: The settings, sorted
---

OPTIONS now stands in five marked groups — CONTROLS (with KEY BINDINGS first), RIDING, HUD, SOUND and PICTURE — every fader reads its value over its track like every other row reads it over its pips, pointing at any row says what it does, and the pause card keeps its settings behind an OPTIONS panel instead of spreading them between RESUME and MAIN MENU.
