type: Changed
title: The checkpoint marks are lit buoys, not cones
---

Every water gate is now marked by a pair of moulded navigation buoys — a wide yellow float collar riding the waterline, a ribbed cone over it, and a lantern with a ridged glass lens under a black cap — in place of the plain cones. The lantern burns on every gate you still owe and goes out the instant you cross the line, so a course reads at a glance as a chain of lights running away down the coast with darkness closing up behind. After dark each lit lamp throws its own pool and column down the water beside the craft's headlamp and the rounding buoys' lanterns, with the four nearest picked by range. `?scene=gate` stands a run on the approach to a checkpoint to look at all of it.
