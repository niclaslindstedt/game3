type: Changed
title: Nine levels a shore, one race one tricks run all the way up
---

Every campaign shore now runs NINE levels instead of six, and the two games alternate the whole way up it: five races around four tricks runs, so the race both opens and closes a shore. The lapped circuit is still the third rung — it is a kind of track, not a third game — the eighth rung is the longest tricks run the campaign sets, six minutes of it, and the ninth is a race down the worst water its coast has in the last light its season allows: eleven metres of sea on the warm shore's sand, more granite on the line than anywhere else in the campaign on the cold one, eight metres of black water off the polar ice, and the mountain storm on the limestone one's reefs. Twelve new shores were curated for it, three a coast, and the tricks runs now lengthen as they go: two minutes, three, four, six. Every rung still asks more than the one before it.

A board from the old six-rung ladder is moved onto the new one rather than dropped: what was rung four, five and six on a shore is rung five, six and seven now, the same water in the same game, and the rungs the re-cut added start open.
