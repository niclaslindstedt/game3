title: Ghosts on a tricks run and a time trial
---

Your best run on a piece of water now comes back out as a GHOST: a
see-through craft — the hull you set the figure on — riding that shore again
exactly the way you rode it, with the gap to it beside the clock, in points
on a tricks run and in seconds down a course. It rides in the two modes
where there is nobody else out there, and the campaign's tricks levels have
one too: the afternoon the medal was won on, riding beside you while you go
for the next one. It is a picture rather than a rival — the ghost runs its
own game, so the two craft can never touch and nothing it does counts for
anything. Kept on your own machine, and replaced by the run that beats it.
