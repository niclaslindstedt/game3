title: A rider on the saddle
---

A man in a wetsuit, a life vest and a full-face helmet now rides every craft, hands on the grips and boots in the footwells (stood in the tray on the stand-up). He slides back and forward with the lean, hangs into a turn and looks through it, tucks into the wind as the pump opens, is thrown back by the throttle and forward by a rock, rises off the seat in the air and takes a landing in his knees and back. The craft's bars come down to where a real machine's stand, so he can reach them.
