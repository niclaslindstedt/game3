title: The camera holds the rider on the way down
---

A craft dropping off a lip no longer slides out of the bottom of the frame: the boom already turned over a falling rider to look down the drop, but the aim point stayed out on the water ahead at the height the sea was, so the shot pointed over his head and walked him off the bottom edge as the fall steepened. The aim now swings with the boom, which makes a flight a rotation of the whole shot about the craft — the lens tilts down to watch him fall, and at the landing the boom's own swing back through the horizontal brings the horizon back up with it, so the bounce is something the picture does and not just something the boom does. The lens follows him down as readily as it follows him up.
