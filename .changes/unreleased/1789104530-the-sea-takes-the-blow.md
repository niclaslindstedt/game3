A landing, a dive and a capsize now mark the water rather than only the air: the hull knocks a crater in the sea that fills back in, a ring wave rolls out of it, a dive throws a wall of water up over the rider as the bow is stopped, a hull going over throws its sheet off the side it comes down on and the water boils round it while it lies on its back — and the righting splashes again as it comes back onto its bottom. All of it sits on the DETAIL row: nothing of it at LOW, the crater and the boil at MEDIUM, the ring wave and the whole throw at HIGH. A `capsize` scene stages it for the screenshot tool and the ride lab.
