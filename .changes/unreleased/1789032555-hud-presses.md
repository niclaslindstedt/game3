The wind vane has moved under the run clock, where it is read together with the two other facts about how the run is going. The top-right corner is now the minimap hard against the screen's two edges, with the two presses a rider makes mid-run — RESET back to the last gate, and CAMERA to the next view — hung under it as glyph buttons sized off the map itself.
