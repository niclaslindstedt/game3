type: Fixed
title: The sea no longer stands in the footwells
---

The water is drawn as a grid that knows nothing floats on it, and a footwell's floor sits within a few centimetres of the craft's resting waterline — so any chop over a hand high drew the sea straight through that floor and left it standing INSIDE the hull with the gunwale dry either side of it. The craft's cockpit is now cut out of the water: every fragment inside the opening and under the rail is dropped. Over the rail nothing is dropped, so a wave that puts the sea over the gunwale still comes in over the side, and a heeled hull still ships it over the low rail.
