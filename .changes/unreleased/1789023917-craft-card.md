---
type: Changed
title: The craft gets a card of its own
---

Choosing a craft was four names in a row on the start card, which asked you to pick between four hulls you had never seen. It is now a screen: the craft turning on the water at the draft it really floats at, with its rider on the saddle, its top speed and its 0–50 counting up beside it, and four bars — acceleration, top speed, turning, stability — placing it against the rest of the roster. The start card's CRAFT row reads back what is chosen and opens it; `?menu=craft` links straight to it.
