title: The front door now stands over a drone shot
---

The sea behind the main menu is no longer photographed from the rider's own chase camera, which put him in the middle of the screen and under the card. It gets a camera of its own instead: a drone a dozen storeys up, out over the open water on a bearing it finds for itself, holding the rider in whatever band of frame the menu leaves free — the side bands on a desktop window, the strip under the card on a phone. It never sits still: the lens drifts round the rider on two slow cycles, the height, the standoff and the lens all breathe, and it banks a degree or two into its own drift. The picture is finished the way an aerial plate is, softening with distance up the frame, and when the seed has dealt a heavy sky the drone simply flies closer, so the rider is somebody you can see whatever the weather.
