type: Added
title: The gate marks and the rounding buoys, modelled
---

The course's furniture is now modelled in Blender off the code's own lines and drawn from committed models: the gate mark as a smooth moulded float with a rolled bead round its shoulder, true strakes up the cone, a grab bar on the flange and a mooring eye under the foot, its lantern in a cage; the rounding buoy as a steel can with a rolled rim and a welded seam, lifting lugs on the shoulder, a lattice tower with its braces and platform, and the lantern in its cage under a cap. A build can still draw the code's lathes (`VITE_MODEL_MARKS=0`).
