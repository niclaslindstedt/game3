The water now comes back through the phone. The bottom slapping the chop — read straight off the hull's own slam, in g of the craft's weight, so every craft answers the same wave the same way — every landing, the bow burying, a skerry and going over are each felt at the size the hull actually took them, with one pulse at a time so a hammering chop can never truncate the landing it comes down into.
