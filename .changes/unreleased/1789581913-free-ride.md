title: FREE RIDE — your own weather on any shore
---

A fourth tile on the front door, and the first way onto the water with nothing asked of you: no lights, no buzzer, nobody else out there. The gates still stand and the tricks still score — it is the engine's own open rules with a door on them — but it is the one card where the weather is set by hand rather than picked off a ladder of words.

Three of the start card's rows become FADERS on a free ride, and one of them is a row no other card has:

- **WIND** in metres a second, from a flat calm to forty — the worded card's rungs are 4, 12 and 20, so this runs to twice the top of the ladder and well past anything the generator would deal.
- **WIND FROM**, the quarter it blows out of, measured off the coast rather than off the compass: straight in off the open water, along the shore, or right round until it is blowing off the land — at which point the fetch is measured over the land behind it and the sea goes flat however hard the row above is pushed.
- **WAVES**, the groundswell standing off the coast, anywhere from one metre to twenty rather than at the nearest rung of the scale. Twenty metres rolling into a flat blue calm is now something you can ask for.

A free ride is also the only mode that rides a speed class other than stock. The class derives a quicker hull AND paces the course for it, so two riders at two classes are not riding the same race — it belongs where nothing is being compared, and it comes back to the measured modes the day there is somebody to measure a rider against. For the same reason a free ride writes no record: its weather is the rider's own, so no two runs down one shore are the same run, and the card says so where the other three show a best.
