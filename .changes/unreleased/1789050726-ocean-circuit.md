title: Ocean circuits — a lapped track out at sea
---

The generator now builds a second kind of level. An OCEAN CIRCUIT is a closed lap standing wholly in open water — at least 230 m off the nearest shore at every metre of it, with the coast a long way off on one side and no river anywhere — ridden two or three times round. The loop is drawn as a polar curve rather than as a walk that finds its way home, so it closes without a kink and comes out somewhat circular and never a circle; its bends carry SEA STACKS at the centre of the turn, so every lap is ridden round rocks as well as round buoys, and the sea out there is the ocean's own: twenty-five to forty-five metres of water under the hull and the whole coast's fetch upwind of it. The gates are measured out once round at a spacing that divides the lap exactly, one of them flies, and the finish line is the start line. Ask for one with `?track=circuit`, or `TRACK=circuit` on `make level`, `make analyze`, `make sim` and `make screenshots`; the HUD grows a LAP chip when a run has laps in it.
