title: Ocean circuits — a lapped track from the shore, out to sea and back
---

The generator now builds a second kind of level. An OCEAN CIRCUIT is a closed lap ridden two or three times round: it starts at the beach, tracks the shore for a stretch of shallow, shoaling water, turns out into the open sea, rounds what is standing out there and comes back in to cross the line it started on. There is no river on one. The loop is drawn as a polar curve in the sea's own frame — a teardrop with its flat side against the coast and its nose out in the ocean, stretched along the shore so the inshore run is a run rather than a point of contact — so it closes without a kink and its tightest corner is known before a metre of it is built. The gates are measured out once round at a spacing that divides the lap exactly, one of them flies, and the finish line is the start line. Ask for one with `?track=circuit`, or `TRACK=circuit` on `make level`, `make analyze`, `make sim` and `make screenshots`; the HUD grows a LAP chip when a run has laps in it.
