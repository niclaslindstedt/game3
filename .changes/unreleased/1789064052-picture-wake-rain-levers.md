title: The wake and the rain are picture levers
---

The picture's DETAIL row now also decides how much of the wake is drawn (LOW keeps the road as foam on a sea that does not bend for it) and how much rain is in the air and on the water, RESOLUTION's MEDIUM draws half the screen's pixels rather than a little over half, and a sea with no lit buoy on it no longer pays per pixel for four dark lamps.
