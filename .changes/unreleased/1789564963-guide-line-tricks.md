title: The guide line no longer blinks out in a tricks run
---

In a tricks run the dashed line on the water ended at the next lip, and the
lip was read off the hull's nose — so it disappeared over the top of every
flip, through the turn back onto the field, and at either end of it. A run
with no course to count is now given the rider's own stretch of the racing
line, laid in the direction the hull is actually travelling: it runs on past
them whichever way they are riding the field, and a backflip no longer turns
it round.
