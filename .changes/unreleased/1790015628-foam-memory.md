title: The sea is water again
---

The white foam standing on the water is gone. It was drawn by a store that
remembered where the sea had broken, and that store kept the loudest share
over a cell metres across and over seconds of clock at once — so a hand's
width of crest going over painted a whole cell of white water for as long as
the memory ran. On one taiga shore it covered a sheltered bay in 2.9 m/s of
wind about nine tenths white, where the rule behind it asks for under one per
cent. What is white now is what is breaking, where it is breaking: the surf
off the shore, a steep crest spilling, the tops the wind blows off.
