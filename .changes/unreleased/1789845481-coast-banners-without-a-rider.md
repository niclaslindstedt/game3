type: Changed
title: The coast pictures on the shore rows are of the coast alone
---

The banner behind every shore row on the CAMPAIGN and RACE cards no longer has a craft parked in the middle of it, or the guide line's dashes running away from it. The row is asking which COAST, and a hull in the frame answered a different question: it was one arbitrary craft the picker was not choosing there, and it dragged a HUD readout — a dashed line down the middle of the water — into a picture that is meant to be a place. The shot is the same shot otherwise: the same shore, the same hour, the same light, the same lens.
