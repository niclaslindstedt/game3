type: Added
title: The shore's trees, modelled
---

Every tree on every coast is now modelled in Blender off its own row of the roster and drawn from committed models, in six variants a kind so a stand no longer reads as one tree stamped: the taiga's pines with their copper upper trunks and needle pads, spruces of drooping boughs, birches, aspens, rowans and alders in leaf; the mangrove's red mangroves on arching prop roots, coconut palms with feathered fronds, cabbage palms with pleated fans, live oaks and slash pines; the karst's holm oaks, silver olives, cypress columns and coastal pines leaning over the water. Near trees are drawn whole and far ones as light sketches. A build can still draw the code-built trees (`VITE_MODEL_TREES=0`).
