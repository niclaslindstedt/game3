title: The craft and its rider are lit like the water beside them
---

The hull, the deck, the bars and the rider are drawn on a lit surface with a real highlight and the sky in it: every part carries its finish — gel coat, rubber, vinyl, chrome, wet neoprene, a helmet's shell — so the sun runs down a polished flank as the hull rolls while the grips and the mats stay dead, and the shell reflects the very sky the sea reflects, which is what makes a white hull take the glow of a sunrise or a sunset along its rail instead of standing in front of it.
