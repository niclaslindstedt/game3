title: The front door's drone is calmer, and keeps the rider low in the frame
---

Two things the menu's drone was getting wrong. It put the rider in whichever band of screen the card left roomiest, which on a phone with a tall card was the band ABOVE it — the far distance in a shot that looks down, where he is small, hazed and inside the picture's own softening; the floor of the frame now wins unless the ceiling has nearly twice the room. And it framed him where he actually was, every frame, so a hull slamming through a chop moved the whole picture rather than moving within it: the shot is now composed on a point that lags him by about a second and is leashed to him, and the lens prefers to stand astern of the direction he has been riding, averaged over several seconds, so it trails his line instead of holding a fixed bearing while he rides across it.
