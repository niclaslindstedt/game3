---
type: Changed
title: The coast pictures on the shore rows have no course on them either
---

The banner behind every shore row on the CAMPAIGN and RACE cards no longer has the checkpoints in it: the gate marks, the rings and the ramps under them, the rounding buoys, and the lamps any of them throw on the water after dark. A course is one RIDE laid over a coast and a shore is nine of them, so a row that showed one was advertising water the row is not about — the same mistake as putting a single level's layout behind it, and the row already has a layout under it on every box. What is left is the coast: the water, the light, the shore and what grows on it.
