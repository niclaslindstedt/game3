title: The sea forms waves again, and costs what it used to
---

The last change to the wave field went too far the other way: the fan it spread the components over was wide enough that no wave front formed — the surface was texture with no wave in it — and it laid sixteen components where eight had been, which is seven milliseconds a frame the water mesh does not have. The fan is back to the width the game was tuned at (26° at the peak, opening only above it), the count is back to eight, and what stops a sea of eight repeating is now where the eight SIT: the band is cut into slices of equal energy rather than equal frequency, so three or four of them crowd onto the peak and carry one wave train between them whose beat is hundreds of metres long. Wave fronts are twenty metres again, the sea repeats less than it did before any of this, and a frame costs what it did.
