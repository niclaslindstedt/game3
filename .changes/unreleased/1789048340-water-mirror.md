title: The water reflects the shore, the craft and a sky it does not swim under
---

The sea now mirrors what stands over it — the coast and the wood on it, the rocks, the gates, the craft and its rider — under the sky it already reflected, and the drawn water no longer slides along with the craft: the wave grid is laid in nested rings that keep every sample on the same point of the sea from one frame to the next. The sun's glitter is one lobe sized from the wind, the crests are read by what they reflect rather than by a painted tint, and the skyline fades in the mirror the way a rough sea's does. Foam is lace with the water showing through rather than a white sheet, laid in streaks along the wind; a steep face foams only where its top goes over, and whitecaps stand on the crests a fresh wind actually raises. The shore's reflection is part of the DETAIL row, and OFF at its lowest stop.
