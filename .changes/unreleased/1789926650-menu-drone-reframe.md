title: The menu's drone walks to its new framing instead of jumping to it
---

Every page of the front door is a different width, and the drone shot behind
them holds the rider in whatever band of frame the card leaves — so turning a
page asked for a new band and the rider crossed the frame in a single frame,
which read as the picture jumping every time the menu was clicked around in.
He is now flown there over about a second, on a curve that is flat at both
ends, the way the shot's bearing and standoff already move.
