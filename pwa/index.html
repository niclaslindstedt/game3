Sea Haven

A personal-watercraft racing game that runs in your browser. Ride a jet ski along a generated northern shore — bedrock slabs, boulder fields, skerries offshore — through a course of buoy gates and airborne rings, on water built from real wave physics: a wind-driven sea that grows the further out you ride, shoals over the shallows and breaks where it gets too tall for the depth under it. Playable on your phone or desktop, offline once loaded. No account, no download, free.

How it plays

The course is a line of gates. A water gate is two buoys — cross the line between them. An air gate is a ring over the water with a floating ramp moored before it: hit the ramp with pace, lean back, and fly through. There is no brake and no gear, only a throttle and a nozzle — and with no thrust there is almost nothing to steer with, so a turn is taken on the gas. Land level and the hull skips on; land nose-down and it dives.

The craft

Four ship: the skiff, a light runabout that is quick, nimble and skittish in chop; the marlin, heavy performance, the fastest and the one that needs room; the otter, a stable tourer, heaviest and softest over waves; and the dart, a stand-up, lightest and most agile and least stable. Every hull, every wave and every shore is written in code.

Controls

Keyboard: W or the up arrow for throttle, A and D or the arrows to steer, S or the down arrow to lean back, Shift to lean forward, R to reset to the last gate, Enter to restart, C for the camera. Touch: the left half of the screen is the handlebar, the right half the throttle lever — drag down to open it.

Install it

Sea Haven is a progressive web app. On iOS Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android Chrome, menu → Install app. The installed game launches fullscreen and plays offline.
